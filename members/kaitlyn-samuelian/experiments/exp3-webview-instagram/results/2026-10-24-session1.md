## Findings

1. **Instagram's mobile website works in `WKWebView` with the default user agent.** Login persists across app restarts, and the DM inbox, feed, profiles, stories and search all load. Evidence class: Confirmed on our device, for Instagram's website on this date. This answers most of the U4 question; sending DMs and photos still needs checking.
2. **Hiding links by address works.** The Reels tab, the Explore button and profile Reels tabs didn't appear, with no dependence on the interface language.
3. **The swipe rule misses Instagram's Reel viewer.** The rule assumed each new Reel changes the address from one `/reel/<id>/` to another. Since scrolling still worked, Instagram either loads more Reels without changing the address, or uses a different address pattern. A "Recent pages" diagnostic (page types and video starts only, no IDs) was added to find out which.
4. **Instagram shows a "use the app" prompt** inside the web view. Not yet handled; hiding it would depend on page structure, which is fragile.

## Diagnostic: how the extra Reels get in

Opened a Reel a friend sent from a DM thread, then scrolled. ✋ › Recent pages showed:

| Time | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| 2:23:39 | `/direct/inbox/` |
| 2:23:42 | `/direct/t/{id}/` |
| 2:23:49 – 2:24:24 | 7 × "video started", **no address change** |

The screen showed Instagram's Reels player over the chat, labelled **"Suggested"**, with Follow, a music tag, the like/comment/share column and "Send to chat".

**Finding:** Instagram's mobile website opens a shared Reel in an overlay player on top of the DM thread and loads algorithmic "Suggested" Reels as you scroll, **without changing the page address**. Address-based blocking can't see this. Evidence class: Confirmed on our device on this date.

**Fix (version 2):** the page script now watches full-screen videos.
- A full-screen video that starts within 4 s of a tap is allowed (the Reel you opened).
- A different full-screen video that starts without a tap is paused, counted as "Scrolled to another Reel", and the app reloads the chat, which closes the viewer.
- "Full-screen" means at least 80% of the visible height and 90% of the width, so feed posts and in-chat videos aren't affected.

This is a heuristic. A tap inside the viewer (such as mute) followed by a quick scroll could slip through.

## Version 2 test: it works

Counts reset, then a friend's Reel opened from a DM and scrolled, twice. ✋ › Recent pages:

| Time | Entry | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2:33:30 | `/direct/inbox/` | |
| 2:33:31 | `/direct/t/{id}/` | Opened the chat |
| 2:33:37 | ▶︎ full-screen video you tapped | Friend's Reel allowed |
| 2:33:46 | ■ full-screen video reached by scrolling | Suggested Reel blocked; back to the chat |
| 2:33:57 | ▶︎ full-screen video you tapped | Reopening the friend's Reel still works |
| 2:34:05 | ■ full-screen video reached by scrolling | Blocked again |
| 2:34:07 – 2:34:15 | `/direct/inbox/`, `/`, `/{id}/`, `/` | Inbox, home feed, a profile, home feed: no blocks |

Counters: "Scrolled to another Reel" **2**; all others **0**.

**Result:** checklist item 8 now passes. A Reel a friend sent plays, can be reopened, and scrolling into Suggested Reels is stopped and returns to the chat. Browsing the home feed and a profile afterward triggered no false blocks.
