# Experiment 1-R26: Session 1 results

Oct 22, 2026 · @KAITLYN

First build and first device run of the ReplayKit Broadcast Upload Extension. Plan and pass criteria: [experiment README](../README.md) and [shared protocols](../../shared/README.md).

## Bottom line

**Our extension received real, changing Instagram Reels frames the whole time Instagram was in the foreground.** On a short, uninterrupted run (2 min 51 s), every U1 pass criterion we could check was met. The open questions are interruptions (lock, app switches, calls, Low Power Mode), the 30-minute run and battery.

## Findings

1. **Instagram Reels is not hidden from ReplayKit capture.** No frame was blacked out, and the picture changed between almost every kept frame. Evidence class: Confirmed on our device, for this Instagram version on this date. Caveat: the measurements show real, changing pixels, but no image was saved, so we haven't *looked* at a frame. A labelled-thumbnail session (off by default) would confirm visually.
2. **The extension's memory limit looks like ≈ 300 MB, not the ≈ 50 MB our research expected.** `os_proc_available_memory()` reported 293–296 MB available at a 4–7 MB footprint. The 50 MB figure was dev-reported (forum 837850, Fora Soft). Possible reasons: iOS 26 raised it, or the device being attached to Xcode changed it. **To confirm:** repeat a run with Xcode closed and the app launched from the home screen. If it holds, 1-R26b (an on-device model in the extension) has far more room than planned.
3. **A normal stop logs `paused` about 2.6 s before `finished`.** Treat one pause right before a finish as part of stopping, not as an interruption.
4. **ReplayKit delivers about 40–60 frames per second** while Reels plays, and we drop ~99% of them. Measuring 1 frame every 2 s cost almost nothing in memory.

## Setup notes (problems hit and how they were solved)

- **Free Personal Team supports App Groups.** Apple issued provisioning profiles for both targets that include the group. Profiles expire **Oct 29, 2026** (7 days); re-run from Xcode to renew.
- **Xcode 26 needs the iOS platform component even for device builds.** Deleting the iOS 26.1 Simulator runtime to free disk space caused "iOS 26.1 is not installed" for the real iPhone too. Don't remove it. (Disk was full at 118 MB free; space was freed and the runtime re-downloaded once.)
- **Two simultaneous downloads of the platform created an "Unusable / Duplicate" copy.** Only download from one place at a time.
- **The disk-full error interrupted creating the extension target.** The project file was saved, but `BroadcastExtension/Info.plist` and `SampleHandler.swift` weren't written, and `Info.plist` wasn't excluded from Copy Bundle Resources ("Multiple commands produce …/Info.plist"). Fixed by recreating the two template files and unticking `Info.plist`'s target membership.
- **`FrameLog.swift` is shared** by ticking both targets in its Target Membership.
- **Logs can be pulled over USB without exporting**, with `xcrun devicectl device copy from --device <id> --domain-type appGroupDataContainer --domain-identifier group.com.kaitlynsamuelian.Exp1R26 --source frame-log.ndjson --destination <file>`.
- **The project layout differs from the README's "Planned files" table.** Xcode created `Exp1R26/Exp1R26.xcodeproj` with `Exp1R26/` (app) and `BroadcastExtension/` folders. There's no separate `Shared/` folder, and `analysis/summarize.py` isn't written yet; the app computes the summary.
